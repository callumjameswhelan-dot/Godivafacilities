Cleaning shouldn’t be something you have to manage.

Most businesses don’t struggle to find a cleaner. They struggle to keep standards up without chasing. The same missed areas get reported again. Absence cover becomes your problem. Nobody actually owns the service.

Godiva runs cleaning as a managed contract. Requirements are agreed in writing before we start, delivery is checked against them, and one named contact is accountable for the result.

  • Defined requirements

    Every area, task and frequency is set out in a cleaning specification. Both sides know exactly what is included.

  • Regular communication

    Scheduled reviews and a direct line to your contract manager — not a mobile number that goes to voicemail.

  • Quality control

    Work is inspected against the specification. Issues are logged, corrected and reported back to you.
