Cleaning for Coventry workplaces.

Coventry’s commercial property runs from city-centre professional offices around the ring road to business parks, university-linked innovation space and large logistics and manufacturing sites on the outskirts. Each needs a different cleaning programme, and a different schedule.

Godiva surveys the building, writes a specification for it and runs the contract with a named manager, planned cover and inspections against the specification. Early-morning, evening and overnight cleaning are all available, with access and keyholding agreed at mobilisation.

  • Office cleaning

    Daily or scheduled cleaning of desks, meeting rooms, kitchens and floors around your working hours.

  • Communal areas

    Lobbies, stairwells, lifts and shared washrooms in multi-tenant buildings and managed property.

  • Industrial offices and welfare areas

    Office, canteen and welfare space within warehouses, manufacturing and trade premises.

  • Washrooms, windows and consumables

    Added under the same contract and scheduled into the same programme.
