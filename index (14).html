What a Godiva contract includes.

Every contract starts with a site survey and a specification. The specification is the reference point for everything that follows: what gets done, how often, when, and how it is checked.

  • Cleaning specification

    Area-by-area schedule of tasks and frequencies — daily, weekly, monthly and periodic — agreed with you before the service starts.

  • Agreed schedule

    Before hours, after hours, overnight or during the day. Frequency and timing are set around how your workplace operates.

  • Named contract manager

    One person responsible for the service, the team and the standard. Your point of contact for changes, issues and reviews.

  • Planned cover

    Rotas are built so that holiday and absence are covered within the team rather than left to chance.

  • Quality inspections

    Work is checked against the specification. Issues are recorded, put right and reported back so you can see the service is being managed.

  • Reviews

    Regular service reviews to confirm the specification still fits the building, adjust frequencies and pick up anything that isn’t working.

  • Additional services

    Washroom services, window cleaning, periodic deep cleaning and consumables can be added under the same contract and the same contact.

Built to fit the way your workplace runs.

There is no standard package. A 20-person office with a shared kitchen needs a different programme from a multi-tenant building with a reception, lifts and six washroom cores.

The survey establishes the building, the current arrangement and the standard you expect. The specification is then written for that building. Frequencies can change as headcount, occupancy and use change — the contract is designed to be adjusted, not renegotiated.

Typical schedules include daily evening cleans for professional offices, early-morning cleans for customer-facing premises, and weekly or fortnightly programmes for smaller or lower-occupancy sites.

How we start a contract.

  1. 01

  4. 04

    Ongoing management
